FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need a doctor's referral to book a session?

No referral is required for an initial assessment. However, for post-surgical or specific medical conditions, we recommend bringing any relevant medical documents (prescriptions, imaging, or doctor's notes) so your therapist can tailor your program accordingly.

How long is each physiotherapy session?

Initial assessment and treatment sessions typically last 60 minutes. Follow-up sessions may range from 45 to 60 minutes depending on your condition and treatment plan.

Do you offer home service?

Yes. We offer home and condo visits within Metro Manila. This is ideal for patients with limited mobility, post-surgery recovery, or busy schedules. Service area and scheduling are confirmed during consultation.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your condition, goals, and response to treatment. Your therapist will discuss an estimated plan during your initial assessment and adjust as you progress.

What should I wear or bring?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting athletic clothing that allows access to the treatment area. Bring any medical documents, imaging, or prescriptions relevant to your condition.
