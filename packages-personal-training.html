Train-With-A-Friend Rate

Train together at the same time and both receive a discounted rate per package:

  • 10+2 Sessions – ₱6,000 discount per person
  • 16+2 Sessions – ₱7,000 discount per person
  • 20+2 Sessions – ₱8,000 discount per person
  • 30 Sessions – ₱12,500 discount per person

Applicable only when training at the same time, same schedule, and same location.
